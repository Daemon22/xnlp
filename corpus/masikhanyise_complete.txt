Incwadi yethu yenzelwe ukuthetha ubomi bethu bemihla ngemihla.
Ukufunda ngolwimi lwethu kukufunda ubomi bethu bemihla ngemihla.
Ulwimi lwethu lusebenza ngokubhalwa ngamagama abantu bemihla ngemihla.
Iilwimi zethu zixhomekeke kwindlela yokuthetha ubomi bemihla ngemihla.
