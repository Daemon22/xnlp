# S.E.K Mqhayi - Complete Works & Available Sources
## For XNLP isiXhosa Language Model Training

### Author Profile
**Samuel Edward Krune Mqhayi (1875-1945)**
- Born: 1 December 1875, Gqumahashe, Eastern Cape
- Died: 29 July 1945, Ntabozuko, near King William's Town
- Titles: "Imbongi yesizwe jikelele" (Poet of the Nation All Over)
- Known as: "The Shakespeare of Xhosa literature"

## Key Passages for Training (Sample Texts)

### From Ityala Lamawele (Opening)
```
Nangani ndingengcali kwathi ni yamthetho, ndinawo noko amanakani oku6a 
umthetho wasemaXhoseni awahluke nakancinane kowezizwe ezikhanyiselweyo. 
Iintlanga eziMhlophe zithe zakufika kweli lizwe zafumana uku6a aBantu 
6eli lizwe Baphantse uku6a ziincutfhe zomthetho Bonke, namasiko aBo 
asekwe phezu kwezi6akala, 6aza ke 6acuntsula na6o kanoBomi kuloo masiko, 
nakuloo mithetho yesiXhosa.
```

UkuHamba yim... S. E. KTUNE MQHAYI... THE LOVEDALE PRESS...

## Linguistic Features to Emphasize

### Traditional Xhosa Elements in Mqhayi's Work
1. **Izibongo (Praise Poetry)**: Formal poetic structures
2. **Umlingo**: Metaphorical expressions
3. **Izafobe**: Idiomatic expressions
4. **Imbongi traditions**: Praise singer conventions
5. **Traditional law terminology**: ubuxhwele, imithetho
6. **Historical references**: Xhosa chiefs, kingdoms
7. **Christian influences**: Biblical translations

### Orthography Notes
- Mqhayi helped standardize Xhosa orthography
- Some texts use older orthography (e.g., "6" for "bh")
- Modern texts use standardized spelling
